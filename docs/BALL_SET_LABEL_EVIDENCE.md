# Ball-set label evidence and data readiness

**Status: multiple official equipment regimes are confirmed; the transition date and historical row-level coverage remain unresolved.** This document does not identify any numeric equipment-count field as a physical ball-set ID.

## 1. Authoritative evidence: the equipment regime changed or was described differently over time

### 1.1 Initial 2007 procedure described two formal sets

The State General Administration of Sport's “超级大乐透”开奖流程 article, published 2007-06-04 shortly after the game's first draw, states that the host/onsite audience selected one set from **two formal sets of draw balls** for that day's draw:

- https://www.sport.gov.cn/cpzx/n5649/c663435/content.html
- Published 2007-06-04; paragraph 2 of the procedure.

This is contemporaneous official evidence for a two-set procedure at launch.

### 1.2 Official material by 2019 describes three formal sets

The State General Administration of Sport article “全方位升级 体彩开奖场地的‘变与不变’” discusses the 2019 draw-hall upgrade and states that Super Lotto has three sets of formal draw balls, all kept by notaries:

- https://www.sport.gov.cn/n20001280/n20745751/n20767297/c21145964/content.html

A State General Administration of Sport article published 2020-01-03 independently confirms that three sets are held by notaries and that a host/guest draws the set index before a draw:

- https://www.sport.gov.cn/cpzx/n5651/c939578/content.html

The official China Sports Lottery 2019 Social Responsibility Report also describes three sets and their inspection/handling requirements:

- https://www.sport.gov.cn/n315/n20067006/c20260981/part/20260998.pdf
- Printed page 49, section “体彩彩票乐透摇奖球”.

**The exact transition date, the reason for the change, and whether set identities were carried forward or renumbered are not established by these official sources. Secondary result archives point to third-set use by September 2011, but require recovery of the original official draw notices before acceptance.** Do not infer them. The transition occurred after the documented 2007 two-set procedure and no later than the 2019 three-set description, but its exact effective date remains unresolved. A later machine replacement is documented separately and must not be conflated with a ball-set transition.

### 1.3 Official institutional retrospective: per-draw ball-set disclosure began in 2011

A Jiangsu Provincial Sports Lottery Center article published 2011-01-10 states that, starting in 2011, the ball set used for each draw began to be formally disclosed to the public, citing draw announcements supplied by the national Sports Lottery Center. It gives draw 11004 (2011-01-08) as an example and reports set 2:

- https://api.js-lottery.com/wfzq/dlt/xwzx/cms/post-32716.html

This is an official provincial-center retrospective about the disclosure practice and an official institutional report of the 11004/set-2 observation. It is stronger than a commercial aggregator, but it is still not the original per-draw national announcement bytes. Record it as an official-retrospective observation, not as a substitute for the primary-source archive required by LOT-058. It also establishes a promising search boundary: prioritize official per-draw announcement recovery from 2011 onward, while not assuming every historical row is recoverable.

The 11105 and 11119 pages were opened and their full page text confirms the stated draw IDs/dates and “本期使用第：3套摇奖球” wording. They remain C1 secondary records: opening and reading the full page improves confidence in what the secondary publisher reported, but does not change source authority or qualify them as official per-draw evidence. These records materially narrow the search target: the third set may already have been in use by September 2011. However, these pages are not the issuing provincial/national lottery authority's archived original announcement, so they are **discovery leads only**. They must not populate the verified row-level label ledger or establish the formal transition date. LOT-058 should use the draw IDs/dates above as priority recovery targets for matching original official notices. If original official bytes cannot be recovered, retain the records as secondary corroboration only.

### 1.5 Machine replacement is a separate event, not proof of ball-set transition

An official State General Administration of Sport article reports that the original “Venus” Super Lotto drawing machine, first introduced on 2007-05-10, was to be replaced after more than twelve years of service. It says the new machine retained the same drawing method and describes the machine replacement; it does **not** state that the physical ball sets were replaced, added, renumbered, or re-identified at the same time:

- https://www.sport.gov.cn/n20001280/n20745751/n20767297/c21145013/content.html

Accordingly, the machine replacement must not be used as a proxy date for the two-set to three-set change. It also cannot establish continuity of physical ball-set identity across the machine replacement. The 2007 two-set procedure and the three-set description by 2019 remain the current official bounds; the effective transition date and the physical identity mapping remain unresolved.

## 2. Consequences for the historical data model

A single timeless three-class label is not valid for the entire 2007–present dataset based on current evidence.

The evidence ledger must distinguish:
- equipment regime (for example, documented two-set regime, documented three-set regime, unresolved transition/unknown);
- the official per-draw set index within that regime;
- whether the physical identity is known to continue across a regime transition.

Do not assume that “set 1” in the two-set period is the same physical equipment as “set 1” in the three-set period. Only explicit official continuity/renumbering evidence can establish that mapping.

The 2007 source documents the two-set procedure at launch; official material documents a three-set regime by 2019. Secondary archives point to third-set use by September 2011, but do not establish an official effective date. The exact transition remains unresolved. This interval is a required research item, not a date to be guessed.

## 3. What the repository currently establishes

The canonical ingestion schema retains the upstream `lotteryEquipmentCount` value as `source_equipment_count` and `lotteryUnsortDrawresult` as `source_unsort_drawresult`. These are raw source fields, not normalized claims about physical ball-set identity or actual draw sequence.

The legacy candidate audit reports 2,336 rows with equipment code 1, 2, or 3 and 565 rows with code 0/unknown. It also reports 560 missing draw-order values. These counts describe a derived legacy candidate only. The candidate's equipment metadata has not been independently reconciled against immutable official per-draw announcements and must not be used as verified ball-set labels.

The independently cross-checked PickWise snapshot corroborates selected numbers and dates for its overlap with the legacy candidate; it does not validate equipment or physical ball-set metadata.

The canonical official history collector currently consumes draw number, draw date, and selected-number result; it does not yet archive and parse the explicit “本期使用第X套摇奖球” field from per-draw official announcements.

## 4. Required archival coverage and regime audit

1. Enumerate every draw ID in the frozen 2,949-row canonical dataset.
2. Resolve each draw to an official national or provincial result-announcement URL. Preserve exact HTML/PDF response bytes, retrieval timestamp, SHA-256, and parser version.
3. Extract a set index only when the official per-draw source explicitly states “本期使用第X套摇奖球”. A search snippet is discovery evidence only, not an archived row-level source.
4. Prioritize recovery of original official announcements for draws 11105, 11119, 11147, 12047, and 12052, whose secondary mirrors report set indices 3/3/3/3/2. Then find authoritative evidence for the two-set to three-set transition date, including any equipment replacement, renumbering, or continuity statement. If no exact date can be established, retain an unresolved regime interval and exclude it from cross-regime identity claims.
5. Assign separate statuses: `verified_two_set_regime_index_1/2`, `verified_three_set_regime_index_1/2/3`, `unknown_regime`, `missing_official_label`, `conflicting_official_sources`, and `unavailable_record`.
6. Publish a row-level map with draw ID, draw date, regime ID, official index, source URL/hash, parser version, and extraction evidence.
7. Report coverage by calendar year, regime, and source domain; include missingness, conflicts, first/last verified draw per index, longest gaps, and denominators.
8. Manually verify a stratified sample and every conflict against archived source bytes. Independent reviewers must reproduce the mapping without inspecting drawn-number patterns.

Do not map `source_equipment_count` to ball-set identity, even if values happen to be 1/2/3. Do not infer from adjacent draws, draw order, or number patterns.

## 5. Modeling eligibility and product consequence

Do not train three timeless historical models by simply grouping every historical “set 1/2/3” index together. Before fitting, establish the regime boundary and whether identities are continuous. Otherwise, treat regimes as distinct conditional populations and preregister which regime has enough verified history for each model slot.

Do not set minimum sample sizes after seeing model performance. Eligibility must consider:
- minimum number of earlier verified labeled draws at every evaluated target;
- temporal coverage and regime stability;
- effective sample size;
- optimizer convergence and finite normalized probabilities;
- calibration and uncertainty width;
- performance relative to pooled and uniform baselines.

If one or more slots are ineligible, the N → 3N generator remains unavailable. Do not copy pooled output into a missing slot or combine unrelated equipment regimes to manufacture sample size.

## 6. Current decision

- Two formal sets are documented in the official 2007 launch-era procedure.
- Three formal sets are documented in official material by 2019.
- The transition date and physical identity continuity are not yet established.
- Explicit per-draw set indices are demonstrably published in some later official result announcements.
- Complete historical per-draw coverage and reproducible row-level mapping are not yet established.
- `source_equipment_count` remains raw metadata and must not be used as a ball-set label.
- LOT-053 Stage 0 remains incomplete; a timeless three-way historical partition is not approved.
- LOT-051 must exclude all equipment/ball-set fields from its confirmatory feature set.
- N → 3N remains a future gated behavior, not a currently available predictor.

The blocker is now specifically historical regime reconstruction, identity continuity, and row-level source coverage. It does not prevent continued development of the pooled model.


## Appendix A. Canonical row-level source observation

LOT-058 should emit an append-only JSONL ledger with one record per draw × source-version. Keep source evidence class separate from label status and regime assignment.

Illustrative shape (not an actual recovered record):

```json
{
  "schema_version": "lottery.ball-set-observation/v1",
  "observation_id": "<stable-id-from-draw-source-and-body-hash>",
  "draw_id": "11105",
  "draw_date": "2011-09-07",
  "draw_date_original": "2011年09月07日",
  "source": {
    "authority_class": "C1",
    "authority_name": "<publisher>",
    "requested_url": "<exact-request-url>",
    "final_url": "<final-response-url>",
    "redirect_chain": [],
    "retrieved_at_utc": "<ISO-8601>",
    "http_status": 200,
    "content_type": "text/html",
    "body_byte_length": 0,
    "body_sha256": "<sha256-of-exact-body-bytes>",
    "raw_object_ref": "<immutable-object-reference>"
  },
  "parser": {
    "name": "dlt-ball-set-announcement",
    "version": "<parser-version>",
    "evidence_locator": "<DOM-locator-or-text-span>",
    "matched_text": "本期使用第3套摇奖球"
  },
  "reported_set_index": 3,
  "equipment_regime_id": null,
  "observation_status": "secondary_only",
  "conflict_group_id": null,
  "supersedes_observation_id": null
}
```

The example is illustrative and explicitly secondary-only; it is not a verified 11105 label. The same draw/source/body hash must yield the same observation ID. A changed body hash creates a new immutable observation version. reported_set_index is nullable and may be 1, 2, or 3 only when explicitly extracted from the archived body. equipment_regime_id remains null until independently adjudicated.

Evidence class values:
- A1: original national Sports Lottery Center per-draw announcement.
- A2: original provincial Sports Lottery Center per-draw announcement.
- B1: official retrospective/procedural source, not the original per-draw announcement.
- C1: third-party media, aggregator, cache, or mirror.

Observation status values should distinguish at least primary_verified, official_retrospective_only, secondary_only, missing_label, source_unavailable, draw_mismatch, parse_error, and conflict. Only A1/A2 observations with matched draw ID/date, explicit evidence locator, preserved raw bytes, and no unresolved conflict may enter the derived verified-label view. Never infer a label from a source class, neighbouring draw, or source_equipment_count.
